THE BARISTA HANDBOOK

A little friendly rivalry.

Earn the most tips in a five-minute shift. Play solo against friends, or share a café with teammates against another team.

  1. Claim a customer ticket and choose a hot or cold cup.
  2. Brew each espresso shot (2.5 seconds), then add the requested milk pours, syrup pumps, and ice. Each ingredient click adds one portion.
  3. Serve before the customer's 90-second patience runs out. Match all seven recipe details for full accuracy.

Tips: a perfect drink served right away earns $5. The tip shrinks the longer the customer waits, down to $2 at the last second. Each recipe detail you miss lowers it further, so a wrong drink still earns something. Tickets that run out earn nothing.

Teams: equal-sized teams share tickets and tips. Claim locks a ticket; “Pass to teammate” releases it with the drink intact. Inactive claims release after 30 seconds.

Every café gets the same recipe sequence. Most tips wins; exact ties share the win. The shift moves from day to sunset to night. Practice is solo and has no competitive ranking.
